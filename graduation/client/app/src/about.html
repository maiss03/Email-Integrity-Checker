About IntegriMail

Email Integrity Checker is a smart and secure email platform built to protect the authenticity of every message. It ensures that emails remain untampered, alerts users in case of any suspicious changes, and provides a safe space for communication inside and outside organizations. The system focuses on trust, transparency, and ease of use — giving users confidence that every email they send or receive is exactly as it should be.

Smart Emailing with IntegriMail

Our platform gives you the power to send and receive emails safely and efficiently — both inside and outside your organization:

  • Step 1: Users can compose and send emails through an intuitive interface connected to real email servers.
  • Step 2: Messages are delivered using secure protocols to prevent interception or tampering.
  • Step 3: The system supports both internal company emails and external communication with the outside world.
  • Step 4: Each internal message is tracked and verified using integrity checks to ensure it hasn’t been altered.
  • Step 5: Users receive notifications about delivery and message status.

How We Keep Your Data Secure

To protect user data and ensure only authorized access, our system applies multiple layers of security:

  • Step 1: Users must log in using a verified username and password.
  • Step 2: After login, a two-factor authentication (2FA) step adds an extra layer of protection.
  • Step 3: The system monitors login activity and alerts admins of any unusual access.
  • Step 4: Sensitive data is encrypted and securely stored.
  • Step 5: Only authorized personnel can access internal tools through strict role-based access control.

How Integrity Check Works

Every email that passes through our system goes through an integrity verification process. Here's how it works:

  • Step 1: When a user sends an email, the system generates a unique hash (digital fingerprint) of the email content.
  • Step 2: This hash is securely stored in the system’s database alongside the email metadata (timestamp, sender, etc.).
  • Step 3: When the recipient receives the internal email, the system re-generates a hash from the received content.
  • Step 4: The new hash is compared with the original hash stored in the database.
  • Step 5: If the hashes match, the email is verified as authentic. If not, an alert is triggered indicating possible tampering.
